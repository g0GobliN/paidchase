# PaidChase

**Send an invoice. Forget it. We chase until you're paid.**

PaidChase is automatic payment follow-up for freelancers and small businesses. Add an invoice, pick a reminder sequence, and stop thinking about who still owes you money.

Not accounting. Not a CRM. Not another dashboard to babysit. Just polite payment reminders — until the invoice is paid.

---

## Why PaidChase

- **Stop awkward chase emails** — reminders go out on schedule, in your name
- **No spreadsheet gymnastics** — see what's outstanding, overdue, and paid
- **Pause anytime** — stop reminders the moment a client says “it's on the way”
- **Mark paid → chase ends** — future reminders cancel immediately
- **Your data stays yours** — per-account isolation and private invoice files

---

## How it works

1. **Add an invoice** (or attach your own PDF)
2. **Choose a sequence** — Friendly, Standard, or Persistent
3. **PaidChase follows up** before due, on due, and after
4. **Mark it paid** — every remaining reminder stops

---

## Reminder sequences

| Sequence | Cadence |
| -------- | ------- |
| **Friendly** | Due date → +3 days → +7 days |
| **Standard** | 1 day before → due date → +3 → +7 |
| **Persistent** | 3 days before → due date → +2 → +5 → +10 |

Tone stays professional. Never aggressive. Never “legal action.” Just clear, human follow-ups.

## FAQ

**Does PaidChase process payments?**  
No. You get paid outside the app. PaidChase only tracks whether you've been paid and stops chasing when you mark an invoice paid.

**Can I upload an existing invoice?**  
Yes — attach your PDF, or generate a simple one.

**Can I stop reminders?**  
Pause, resume, or cancel any invoice anytime.

**Can I customize emails?**  
Yes — edit every reminder subject and body in your voice, with merge fields for client and invoice details.

**Is my data private?**  
Yes. Accounts are isolated; invoice files live in private storage.
